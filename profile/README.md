# Lewis Australia

We're an automation engineering company in Moorabbin, Victoria, with over 40 years of experience designing special purpose production equipment. Our clients in mining, metals, automotive and industry come to us for jobs that no standard machine can do.

## Products

- **ScOREpion**: mobile blasthole sampling
- **Line 5**: lab sampling automation
- **Haul Truck Wheel Changer**
- **Exolance**: automated oxy lancing

## Open source

Our software team runs its own Forgejo. We publish the tools we build for it here, like Forgejo Actions and deployment templates, once they're useful to people outside Lewis.
